About Kaibo audio

Sound. Connection. Life.

Founded in Texas in 2020, on a simple belief: whether it is the raw sound of nature or carefully crafted music, sound is the medium that connects our daily lives, our travels and the world around us.

Our story

From one pair of earbuds to three ways of listening.

Kaibo audio is built on the continuous research and application of bone-conduction technology: taking what the laboratory proves and folding it into something a person is willing to wear all day. The goal is to protect hearing rather than wear it down.

2020

Founded in Texas.

Kaibo audio was born out of a simple desire to combine a passion for music and travel while staying present to what is going on around us. Our inaugural product, Kaibo Buds, was one of the first true bone-conduction headphones in an earbud style.

2022

Crowdfunded, over $700,000 raised.

We launched our first crowdfunding campaign with Kaibo Flex, another first-of-its-kind bone-conduction device with a portable charging dock. The campaign raised over $700,000 and taught us lessons that made the company better and stronger. We remain grateful for the support the crowdfunding community showed us.

2023 and beyond

A growing collection, still open-ear.

With a growing range of both premium and accessible bone-conduction headphones, we stayed committed to products that combine open-ear audio quality and unique features at a price people can reach.

What we make

Three core listening experiences.

Open, high-quality listening means something different in a living room, on a guided tour and across an ordinary day. Each of the three is built for its own situation rather than adapted from the others.

TV Hearing Assistance

Next-generation LE Audio and Auracast™ broadcast, so the television at home arrives low-latency and crystal clear without disturbing anyone else in the room.

Lightweight wireless open-ear headphones built for travel: guide commentary comes through clearly while the traveller stays naturally aware of their surroundings.

What we stand for

Innovation, accessibility, connection.

We work with authenticity, humility and responsibility, and treat every Kaibo user with that same care. These three words are the ones we hold the products to.

Innovation

Kaibo continuously pushes boundaries and introduces innovative solutions so that customers can make the best use of the technology available to them.

Accessibility

As technology progresses, so should accessibility. High-quality audio experiences should be available to everyone, not only to the people who can pay the most for them.

Connection

Kaibo strives for harmony between a personal listening experience and the life going on around it. With that connection, we can truly listen while life happens.
