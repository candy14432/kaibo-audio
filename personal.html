Personal hearing assistance

Life Listening!

Kaibo audio builds for the people who have spent too long on the quiet side of a room. Hearing is not the end in itself. It is the way back to conversation, to confidence, and to doing things on your own terms.

01 — The family table

Everyone talking at once.

A family table is the hardest room there is, with several voices going at the same time, cutlery, someone answering from the far end. The headset lifts whoever you are facing above all of it, so you follow the thread instead of asking twice.

02 — Cafés & terraces

A busy terrace, one conversation.

Traffic, the table behind you, the machine at the counter. Nothing here needs turning up: the headset holds on to the voices at your own table and lets the street stay where it is.

03 — At home

Your own volume, back again.

A record on, a book open, nobody to ask to repeat themselves. Sound arrives at the level you set it, and the rest of the house stays as quiet as it wants to be.

Technology

Auracast, built into the hearing aid.

Ozen Pro is a professional hearing aid and Ozen Buds a simpler PSAP, and both carry Bluetooth LE Audio with Auracast. A broadcast put out by a lounge, a bar or a guide arrives in the ear directly, with no second receiver to carry.

Received without a headset

Where the TV and Tour ranges hand out a receiver, Ozen already is one. The channel is picked up by the aid the wearer has on anyway.

Tuned by AI, then by you

A hearing test in the Ozen app sets the profile, and Ozen AI optimises gain, compression and frequency shaping from it. An EQ adjusts tone and balance from there, over six channels of wide dynamic range compression.

Products

Two ways to hear, and the app that tunes them.

Ozen Pro is the professional hearing aid; Ozen Buds is a simpler PSAP for someone starting out. Both carry Bluetooth LE Audio with Auracast, and both are set up and adjusted from the Ozen app on Apple or Android, hearing test included.

Hearing aid

Ozen Pro

The professional aid has a behind-the-ear body with the receiver sitting in the canal, and Bluetooth LE Audio with Auracast built in, so a broadcast reaches it with no headset in between.

Ozen Buds

The simpler PSAP, for someone starting out. Worn in the ear and charged in its case, carrying the same LE Audio and Auracast as the Pro.

Open-ear buds

An ear hook that leaves the canal clear, so the room around the wearer stays audible alongside whatever is being sent to them. Charged in a metal case.
